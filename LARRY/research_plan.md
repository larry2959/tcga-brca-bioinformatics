# Research plan — lead-owned

## Research question
Which genes differ between breast cancer and normal breast tissue, and can expression profiles separate the two?

## Objectives
1. Retrieve open-access TCGA-BRCA RNA-seq data.
2. Preprocess counts, run QC and exploratory analysis.
3. Test gene-level differences with multiple-testing correction.
4. Train and evaluate tumour-vs-normal classifiers.
5. Interpret results cautiously and document limitations.

## Hypothesis
Tumour and normal breast tissue show distinguishable expression profiles, and a subset of genes has different expression after multiple-testing correction.

## Boundaries
This is exploratory analysis of a public cohort. Statistical association is not causation; classification performance is not clinical validation.
